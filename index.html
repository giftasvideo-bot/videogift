How it works:

  • For Givers: Steps to send video
    • 1. Scan the qrcode or enter the id number in text box and click Go button.

    2. Write your name(optional), Your message(optional) and select the video you want to send and click UPLOAD VIDEO GIFT button. Make sure that once the video is uploaded you can’t change it. Only one video is allowed to upload.

    3. After your video uploaded successfully a message will appear ‘🎉 Your video gift has been successfully uploaded!’ which shows the success of your video upload

    4. Once you have finished give your present to your loved ones either in paper form (printed qr code) or send it digitally to ur loved once electronically.
  • For Receivers:
    • 1. Scan the qrcode or enter the id number in text box and click Go button

    2. The system play back the video gift automatically
