🌿 About Us

At WordGift, we bridge the gap between tangible handcrafted design and digital storytelling. Our signature service transforms physical postcards into interactive keepsakes; by scanning the custom QR code, you can easily upload a personal memorial video that helps express your deepest feelings and most meaningful memories.

Whether you are celebrating a special milestone or honoring a beautiful memory, our physical-to-digital ecosystem ensures your genuine warmth and heartfelt expressions are preserved securely and delivered seamlessly to your loved ones.

We offer our video-gift QR codes in two forms: as a standalone QR code sticker that you can paste onto any physical gift — flowers, a wrapped present, a mug, anything you'd like to attach a personal video to — or as one of our signature printed postcards, ready to write on and hand over as a complete keepsake on its own.

🚀 How to Send

  1. Get your card ready. Every physical Forever 27 card (QR sticker or postcard) has its own unique QR code — this is the permanent link between your video and that one specific card, so make sure you're using the right card for the right person.
  2. Open the upload page. Scan the card's QR code with your phone's camera, or manually enter the card's code (format NNNNNN-AAAA, e.g. 000003-ZEAH). Either way brings you to the upload form for that card.
  3. Add your name and message — both optional. Your name (e.g. "Mum," "The Johnson Family") lets the recipient know who it's from, and a short written message can go alongside your video.
  4. Choose how to add your video. Pick "📁 Upload a File" if you already have a video saved, or "🤳 Record a Selfie Video" to record right there in the browser.
  5. Add the video. For a file: choose it from your device. For a selfie recording: enable the camera, start recording, then stop when done — retake any time before you're happy with it.
  6. Review it. A video preview lets you watch it back and confirm it's the one you want — this is your last check before sending.
  7. Tap "Upload Video Gift." A progress bar shows the upload in real time, and a success message confirms it worked — plus how many upload attempts remain, since each card allows up to 3 attempts in case something goes wrong.
  8. Hand over the card. The physical card is now the key. Once your recipient opens and watches it, the gift is sealed and the video can no longer be replaced — so make sure step 6 is the version you're happy with.
