🌿 About Us

At WordGift, we bridge the gap between tangible handcrafted design and digital storytelling. Our signature service transforms physical postcards into interactive keepsakes; by scanning the custom QR code, you can easily upload a personal memorial video that helps express your deepest feelings and most meaningful memories.

Whether you are celebrating a special milestone or honoring a beautiful memory, our physical-to-digital ecosystem ensures your genuine warmth and heartfelt expressions are preserved securely and delivered seamlessly to your loved ones.

We offer our video-gift QR codes in two forms: as a standalone QR code sticker that you can paste onto any physical gift — flowers, a wrapped present, a mug, anything you'd like to attach a personal video to — or as one of our signature printed postcards, ready to write on and hand over as a complete keepsake on its own.
